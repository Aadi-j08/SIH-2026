# Image Prompts & Visual Aesthetics Guide

Documentary photograph, not an illustration. Shot on a 35mm lens, Kodak Portra 400 look: soft natural light, slightly overcast post-monsoon September afternoon in a middle-class neighbourhood of Bhopal, India. Warm, muted colours — off-white lime-washed walls, terracotta and rust tones, dusty moss green, one small touch of faded indigo. Candid, unposed, subject not looking at the camera. Real textures: worn tools, chipped paint, creased cotton, dust, uneven skin, ordinary faces. Slight film grain, gentle vignette, imperfect framing. No text, no logos, no watermarks, no captions, no glossy HDR look, no over-saturation, no perfect symmetry, hands anatomically correct.
